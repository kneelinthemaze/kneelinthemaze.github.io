An about page, trying to give you context of who I am, where I am, what I am. 

I’m Alex. I live in Australia and I had been a Christian for around a year before I quit, and I’ve recently returned as of April 2026.

“How do I believe in Jesus?”
“Why should I believe in Jesus?”
I want to help you figure those out. I want a say in how you choose to deal with God. I want to give you something relatable and accurate for that purpose. That’s why this blog exists.

Expect a post every Sunday at 9am as shown by the timer on the front page. It will be essays on faith, works, and believing. Usually at around 1200 words. Also some reflections on my personal struggles and experiences. 

I’m inviting you to connect with me next week, or right now. Maybe find a post that resonates with you or wait for the next release. Let me help you. 

Finally, what is “Kneeling in the maze”?

I think that’s what faith feels like. More beautiful than it seems though. 

> 1 Thessalonians 5:21 (NASB 1995): “but examine everything; hold firmly to that which is good;”
